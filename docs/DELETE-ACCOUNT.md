# Delete your AllisWell account

**Languages:** **English** · [Türkçe](DELETE-ACCOUNT.tr.md)

This page explains how to delete your AllisWell account and the data that goes
with it. You do not need to install or open the app to read it, and you can ask
for deletion without the app (see "Without the app" below).

AllisWell is provided by **BUBIAPSS BILGI TEKNOLOJILERI ARGE LIMITED SIRKETI**
(trading as **BubiApps**).

## In the app

1. Open AllisWell and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings ▸ Delete account**.
3. Confirm.

The same option is in the web app at <https://alliswell.space/app>, so you can
also do this from any browser.

## Without the app

If you cannot sign in, or no longer have the app, e-mail **info@bubiapps.com**
from the address you signed up with and write "Delete my account". We confirm
the request and process the deletion for you.

## What happens next

1. Deletion is **scheduled with a 3-day grace period**. Nothing is erased yet.
2. If you change your mind, sign in within those 3 days and cancel the deletion.
   Everything continues as normal. Asking to delete again does not extend the
   original deadline.
3. After 3 days, your account and its content are **permanently and irreversibly
   erased**.

## What is deleted

- your account: name, e-mail address, password and sign-in sessions;
- tasks, projects, notes, tags, folders, checklists and reminders;
- files you uploaded, in every workspace you own;
- connected calendars and AI providers: their stored tokens and keys;
- the devices registered for notifications.

There is no backup copy we can restore for you afterwards.

## What is kept, and for how long

- A file you uploaded into a workspace **owned by someone else** belongs to that
  workspace and stays with it.
- Events copied into your Google Calendar stay there; delete them in Google
  Calendar.
- Request logs are kept only as long as we need them to operate and secure the
  service.

The full details are in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md#deleting-your-account).
